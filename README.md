## 🚀 About Me

I build **production-ready, cross-platform business apps** with Flutter — one codebase shipping to **Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, Linux and Web**.

- 🧭 **16 years in operations & production management** before software — I understand how real businesses run, and I build software that fits them.
- 💼 Focus: **POS systems, inventory & shop management, business dashboards**
- 🏗️ Clean architecture: **MVC / GetX bindings, repository & service layers**
- ☁️ Backends: **Supabase** (Postgres, Auth, Storage, Realtime) & **Firebase**
- 📴 Offline-first local storage with **Hive**
- 🤝 Available for **freelance projects & long-term collaborations**
